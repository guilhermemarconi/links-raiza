# Links Raíza

Link-in-bio page for Raíza Souto. A visitor arriving from a social profile sees who she is and picks where to go next: one of her three products, or another destination.

## Language

**Bio page**: the single public page, opened from Raíza's social profiles, that presents her and routes visitors elsewhere.
_Avoid_: Landing page, site, home

**Profile**: the introduction at the top of the Bio page: photo, name (Raíza Souto) and a short description, in that order.
_Avoid_: Header, hero, about

### Products

**Product**: something Raíza sells that the Bio page presents. There are three: COLUNA, PILAR and Work Session. No price is ever shown.
_Avoid_: Offer, service, plan

**COLUNA**: subscription for people who want access to Raíza's worldview, on subjects for one's life and for one's company.

**PILAR**: individual annual mentoring with Raíza and the Ohbra team.

**Work Session**: two one-off meetings with Raíza: the first to understand the person's scenario, the second to deliver based on that need.

**Product card**: the clickable presentation of one Product. Its image carries the pitch; a written description exists alongside it for accessibility. A Product without a destination is registered but its card is not shown.
_Avoid_: Banner, button

Products carry equal weight; their order on the page is set by the editor.

### Other destinations

**Extra link**: a destination that is not a Product, such as a content channel, shown as a titled link with an optional thumbnail.
_Avoid_: Link (alone, ambiguous with a Product card's destination), button

**Social link**: a link to Raíza's presence on a social network, shown as an icon. The networks are Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and LinkedIn; only Instagram has an address today.
_Avoid_: Social icon, network button

### Measurement

**Destination click**: a visitor's click on a Product card, a Social link or an Extra link. It is the only interaction the Bio page measures, together with the page view.
_Avoid_: Conversion (nothing is bought on the page), hit
